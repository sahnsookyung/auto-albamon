Jobs that passed the automated date, schedule, pay, commute, worksite, and exclusion rules. Some can still require a manual posting-condition check.

Manual review queue

Either a posting condition JobScout cannot confirm—such as eligibility, exact worksite, or a certificate—or a job you deliberately deferred with “Maybe later.”

Resolving a review: open the job and complete the stated check. Marking it complete removes that exact evidence version from this queue; changed evidence reopens it.
